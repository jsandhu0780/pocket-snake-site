Privacy policy

Pocket Snake for iPhone · Last updated November 9, 2026

The short version

Pocket Snake does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your stars, best scores and settings (such as the colour look and sound on or off) on your iPhone so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device. We can't see it, and deleting the app deletes it.

What we collect

Nothing. Pocket Snake makes no network requests and includes no third-party code that collects data.

Children

Because the game collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
