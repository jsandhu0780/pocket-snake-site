Help for the iPhone game

How do I play Parade?

Drag anywhere on the screen to steer. People you touch join your parade. Pass through a glowing party zone to score everyone in your line. Bigger parades score far more. Crossing any parade cuts it, so keep your line away from rival snakes.

How do I open the next park?

Finish first in a park. Score higher to earn the second and third stars.

Where is the classic snake game?

Tap Classic on the title screen. It has 30 stages and an Endless mode. Tap Back to Parade to return.

There's no sound

Pocket Snake follows your silent switch. Turn silent mode off and check the volume. In Classic, the Sound button also turns sound on and off.

Will I lose my progress?

Progress is saved on your iPhone. It stays when you update the app, but deleting the app deletes it.
